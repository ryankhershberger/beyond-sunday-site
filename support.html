Help with the Beyond Sunday app for iPhone and iPad.

Adding a sermon

How do I add my church’s sermon? Open Sermons, tap +, and choose Search Podcasts. Search for your church or pastor, open the show, and pick the message. If your church isn’t listed, use Paste Podcast Link, upload an audio file, paste a transcript, or record the sermon.

Why can’t I use a Spotify link directly? Spotify doesn’t allow other apps to download its audio. Beyond Sunday looks for the same episode in the publisher’s own public podcast feed, which most churches also have.

Transcription is slow or didn’t finish. Sermon audio is turned into text on your device. A long sermon can take several minutes, and the first time may need a one-time download of Apple’s speech model. Keep the app open and the device charged, then tap Retry on the sermon if it stopped.

Studies and AI

What is a “study writing”? One AI-written Bible study, including the sermon summary it needs. Your first study writing is free, once per device. After that, Beyond Sunday AI is a subscription with a set number of writings each month.

I wasn’t happy with a study. Was I charged a writing? A writing is used only when a study is created successfully. If it fails or you cancel, it isn’t used. You can edit any study, regenerate a section, or replace a question afterward.

Can I use the app without AI? Yes. You can import and read transcripts, see the Scripture a sermon refers to, and build a complete, timed study from the starter template.

Where does the Bible text come from? Every passage is taken from the World English Bible stored in the app. Scripture is never written by AI.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I cancel or change my plan? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Profile › AI Service › Plans & Writings › Manage Subscription, or go to the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but the app says I have no plan. Open Profile › AI Service › Plans & Writings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Your data

Where is my data stored? On your device, and in your own private iCloud account if iCloud sync is on. There is no Beyond Sunday account to create.

How do I delete my data? In the app, go to Profile › Storage & privacy › Erase All Data. To have Beyond Sunday AI plan records deleted from our service, email us at hershapplications@gmail.com.
