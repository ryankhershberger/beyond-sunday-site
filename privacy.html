Beyond Sunday Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Beyond Sunday ("the app") is published by Hersh Applications ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • The app works without an account.
  • Your sermons, studies, groups, and journal entries are stored on your device (and in your private iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync).
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Sermon text or audio is sent to an outside service only if you (or your church) configure one and allow it.

Information stored on your device

  • Sermons you import or record: titles, speaker and church names you enter, transcripts, audio files, summaries.
  • Bible studies, edits, private leader notes, groups, and meeting schedules.
  • Personal journal entries and reflection progress.
  • Preferences (study defaults, reminders, appearance).

You can delete any item, delete downloaded audio, or erase all data in Profile › Storage & Privacy.

iCloud sync

Unless you turn it off in Profile, your data syncs between your devices through your private Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We cannot access your private iCloud data.

On-device processing

Where your device supports it, speech transcription (Apple Speech / SpeechAnalyzer) runs entirely on your device. AI summaries and studies are created by the AI service you choose to connect (see below). On some older devices, speech recognition may use Apple's servers only if you allow it in Settings; Apple's handling is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Finding sermons

When you search for a podcast, your search words are sent to Apple's public podcast directory, and the app then downloads the show's public feed and the episode you choose directly from the church or publisher that hosts it. Those services see the request the same way they would from any podcast app. We don't receive your searches or what you listen to.

AI services you connect (optional)

If you connect your own AI service account (for example Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI) in Profile › AI Service, the app sends the sermon transcript, its summary and related Bible passages directly from your device to that provider when you summarize a sermon or create a study. Your API key is stored only in your device's Keychain. Your journal and leader notes are never sent. That provider handles the content under its own terms and privacy policy for your account; we never receive it.

Beyond Sunday AI (optional)

If you use Beyond Sunday AI, including the free first study writing or a subscription, the sermon transcript, its summary and related Bible passages are sent to our service when you summarize a sermon or create a study. The service uses Anthropic's Claude to write the study and doesn't store your sermon text. Your journal and leader notes are never sent. To track your plan and remaining writings we keep a random account ID created by the app (there's no sign-up), your App Store transaction IDs, plan dates and usage counts. The account ID is stored in your iCloud Keychain so your plan follows you to your other devices. So that the free first study writing is given once per device, the app sends our service an Apple DeviceCheck token, which lets us ask Apple whether that device has already used it. The token doesn't identify you or your device to us. We don't receive your name, email, Apple ID or payment details; Apple processes payments. To have this account information deleted, contact us at hershapplications@gmail.com.

Web links for studies

When you create a read-only web link for a study, only the participant version of that study is stored on our service so that people with the link can view it. Your leader notes are never included. You can turn the link off at any time, which deletes it.

Permissions

The app asks for the microphone (recording a sermon), speech recognition (transcription), and notifications (reminders) only when you use those features.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Content rights

The app processes only content you provide or that is publicly distributed in podcast feeds. Please only import material you have permission to use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: hershapplications@gmail.com
